ML Probe test

  1. Turn the phone's volume all the way up.
  2. On the watch, open ML Probe.
  3. Lay the phone flat on a table. Lay the watch on the screen, on the bottom third, near the speaker. Don't touch either during the test.
  4. Tap Start. When it says GO, press the watch's START button once.
Ready
About 2 minutes

Then, for the Bluetooth part: lock the phone, wait 5 seconds, unlock it. Do that 3 times. Afterwards, plug the watch into the PC.
