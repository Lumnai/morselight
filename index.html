Morse Light

Type a message for your watch.

A–Z, 0–9, . , ? / and spaces0/48

    Scan the code on your watch.

    On the watch open Morse Light › Phone, then point your phone camera at the QR code.

    Keep Morse Light open on the watch; a message shows up within about 10 seconds, and one sent up to 5 minutes before you open it still arrives.

    No signal? Messages wait on this phone and send by themselves once it is back online. A Flash now that waits more than 10 minutes goes out as Save on watch, so the watch never starts flashing unexpectedly. Add this page to your Home Screen so it also opens with no signal.

    Messages are encrypted on this phone with a key from the watch code, then passed through the free ntfy.sh relay, which cannot read them.
